Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to common questions regarding local storage, AI features, subscriptions, and backups. Need personal assistance? We are one email away.

All your notes are stored directly in your phone's internal application database. You do not need an internet connection or an active account to create, edit, search, or read notes. Everything functions 100% offline.
Because Mnemo does not store a mirror copy of your notes on a remote cloud server, clearing application data in your Android settings or uninstalling the app will erase your locally stored notes. We strongly advise using the built-in backup/export features regularly to keep archive copies on your SD card, Google Drive, or PC.
Mnemo supports an optional on-device language model for supported phones with compatible hardware and RAM. Not every AI operation runs locally—certain deep reasoning features in the Ask and Memory sections utilize secure external AI endpoints only when explicitly invoked by you. We never train public models on your private notes.
Never. Your notes remain on your phone regardless of subscription status. Canceling Premium simply turns off premium AI quota and power text operations; it never locks, restricts, or deletes your note archive.
Desktop editions are on our active product roadmap. We are currently focusing on ensuring the Android release is exceptionally stable, after which preview builds for Windows, macOS, and Linux will be announced to our early access community.
