Privacy Policy for Mnemo

Application: Mnemo (Android)
Contact: rish.jobsearch@gmail.com
Last Updated: October 2026

Executive Summary: Mnemo is an offline-first, sovereign personal knowledge application. Your notes, titles, Markdown text, photo notes, and precision drawings are stored 100% locally on your device. We do not sell, scrape, or transmit your private content.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Mnemo is architected around complete user privacy and local-first boundaries:

  • No Cloud Accounts Required: You do not need to register with an email address or password to write and organize notes.
  • Zero Content Tracking: Your notes, folders, tags, Markdown documents, photos, drawings, and local search embeddings remain exclusively on your phone's private SQLite sandbox.
  • No Content Transmission: We do not operate proprietary cloud servers that upload, parse, or train machine learning algorithms on your written thoughts.

2. Third-Party Services & Google Play Integration

To deliver in-app subscription management and advertising monetization, Mnemo integrates standard Google Play developer SDKs:

Google Play In-App Billing

When you purchase a Premium subscription (Monthly, Quarterly, 6-Month, Annual, or Lifetime), transactions are processed directly by Google LLC via Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card information, bank accounts, or billing addresses. Transactions are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

During introductory free periods, Mnemo may display non-personalized or personalized advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, general device type, and crash diagnostics to prevent fraudulent clicks and monitor ad delivery. You can opt out of personalized ad targeting at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Upgrading to any Premium plan permanently turns off all advertisements.

3. AI Architecture & Clear Distinctions

Mnemo features two distinct AI paradigms:

  • Optional On-Device Language Model: On supported devices with hardware compatibility, machine intelligence runs directly on your smartphone's chipset without transmitting text to external servers.
  • Connected AI Queries: Certain advanced semantic query features (in Ask and Memory) communicate with verified secure inference endpoints solely when you trigger a query. Your input is processed transiently and is never used to train public models.

4. Android Device Permissions

Mnemo requests minimal permissions only when you explicitly invoke specific tools:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used only when you choose to capture a picture for a Photo Note. Captured images are saved locally to your device.
  • Media / Photos: Used only when you manually pick an image from your device gallery to attach to a note.
  • Internet Access: Required strictly to verify Google Play license receipts and render connected queries when requested. Note contents are never uploaded passively.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Because your database lives in your device's sandboxed storage, you possess absolute authority over deletion:

  • Instant Note Purge: Deleting a note in Mnemo immediately purges it from your local SQLite database.
  • Complete Erasure: Clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling Mnemo permanently erases all local data. We strongly advise using the built-in Export feature regularly to maintain offline backups.
