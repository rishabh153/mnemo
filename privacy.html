Our Core Promise

Your thoughts are personal. Your app should be too.

We designed Mnemo around a simple non-negotiable principle: you own your notes. We do not hold your thoughts hostage behind subscriptions, proprietary lock-in, or mandatory cloud accounts.

Local-First Storage

Your notes are stored on your device's local database. They remain accessible whether you are offline in airplane mode or have no internet connection.

Zero Subscription Ransom

Unlike cloud services that lock your database behind a paywall when you cancel, your Mnemo notes stay in your device storage unconditionally.

Full Portability

Easily export notes as standard Markdown, structured JSON, or high-definition PDFs. Take your knowledge anywhere without proprietary traps.

Transparent Engineering

Clear facts on how Mnemo handles AI

Too many modern software products hide their data pipeline behind ambiguous "AI" marketing. We believe in absolute clarity.

1. Optional On-Device AI

Mnemo supports integration with a local language model executing directly on your phone's processor.

  • • Runs on supported modern hardware with sufficient RAM.
  • • Computations happen on-device without cloud transmission.
  • • Completely optional: You choose whether to download or enable it.

2. Hybrid & Connected AI Features

For deeper semantic queries and complex summaries, Mnemo connects to trusted AI providers only upon explicit user action.

  • • We do not scrape, sell, or train models on your private notes.
  • • Queries are processed transiently with strict access control.
  • • We do not claim all AI runs locally: we keep the distinction honest.

An Honest Note on Local Storage & Backups

Because Mnemo prioritizes your privacy by keeping your database on your phone rather than holding a mirror copy on a remote company server, your device is the single source of truth.

If you clear the application data in Android settings, format your device, or uninstall the app without an export, your locally stored notes will be erased.

Recommended Best Practices:
  • Export periodic backup archives using Mnemo's built-in export tool.
  • Store backup files on your preferred secure drive, SD card, or computer.
  • Export finished writing and documents as PDF or Markdown.
