Complete Feature Guide

Built for power thinkers who demand control

Every capability in Mnemo has been designed to eliminate clutter and provide genuine utility. Explore how our tools empower your daily writing and knowledge synthesis.

Writing & Editing

Rich Markdown & Typography

Write with the speed of plain text and the polish of professional publishing. Mnemo delivers an intuitive Markdown engine with realtime rendering.

  • Full Markdown support: headers, bold, italics, checklists, blockquotes, and code fences.
  • Live word count, character count, and reading duration estimates.
  • One-tap formatting bar designed specifically for thumb ergonomics on Android.
Productivity Utilities

Text operations inspired by desktop power editors

Mobile text editors are usually crippled by simplistic interactions. Mnemo integrates power workflows popularized by utilities like Notepad++.

Case Conversions

Instantly toggle selected text between UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, and Sentence Case without tedious manual retyping.

Line Sorting & Deduplication

Sort lines alphabetically, reverse ordering, or strip duplicate entries from your lists with a single tap.

Whitespace Cleansing

Trim trailing whitespaces, collapse excess empty lines, and normalize indentation across messy pasted text.

Organization & Speed

Find anything in milliseconds

When notes multiply, organization makes or breaks your productivity. Mnemo gives you a robust taxonomy of folders, categories, and ultra-fast SQLite search.

  • Nested Folders & Tags: Group research into logical projects.
  • Pinned Priority: Keep critical scratchpads and active checklists pinned to top.
  • Unified Search: Search terms, tags, and content across your entire archive instantly.
Visuals & Documents

Photo notes, precision drawing & PDF exports

Not all thinking happens in words. Capture diagrams, annotate photos, sketch ideas with stylus precision, and export publication-ready documents.

  • Annotate photos with colored pens, highlighters, and vector callouts.
  • Export clean, styled PDF documents with custom margins for easy printing and sharing.
  • Local asset storage ensures your images remain high-resolution and uncompressed.
Focus Mode

Lite Mode: Distraction-free clarity

Prefer absolute simplicity? Toggle Lite Mode in settings to strip away secondary controls and experience an ultra-clean, minimalist writing environment.
