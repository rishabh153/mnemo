Meet Mnemo

A more personal way to think, write and organise

Experience an interface built for clarity and speed. From deep nested categories to live Markdown and AI recall, Mnemo adapts to how your mind works.

Clean, Organised Note-Taking

Structure your thoughts with nested folders, categories, pinned notes, and instant access to your recently used ideas without cluttered distractions.

  • Smart categorisation and tag-based grouping
  • Pin priority notes to the top of your feed
  • Recent notes quick-switcher bar

Core Capabilities

Engineered for control, productivity and speed

Every feature in Mnemo has been built to eliminate friction. You get power-user utilities combined with a refined, tactile interface.

Folders & Categories

Organise projects with flexible folder trees, colour-coded categories, and tag filtering to maintain order across thousands of notes.

Rich Markdown & Typography

Enjoy clean Markdown rendering, headings, code syntax, tables, and task checklists with customized typography tailored for long-form reading.

Power Text Operations

Inspired by tools like Notepad++: perform instant case conversions, line sorting, whitespace trimming, and duplicate removals with a single tap.

Photo Notes & Precision Drawing

Embed high-resolution images, draw freehand diagrams, annotate screenshots with vector tools, and keep visual research attached to your notes.

PDF Export & Clean Sharing

Export formatted notes into crisp, publication-ready PDFs with custom margins and preserved typography for seamless archiving and sharing.

Lite Mode & Workspace Themes

Prefer minimalism? Switch on Lite Mode for an ultra-lean distraction-free writing slate, or customize themes to match your creative flow.

Intelligence & Sovereign Privacy

AI that respects your boundaries

We believe intelligence should serve your thinking, not compromise your security. Mnemo treats your notes as your personal property.

Transparent AI Architecture

We make a strict distinction between optional on-device machine intelligence and connected AI services so you always know where your data lives.

Local & Private

Optional On-Device Model

On compatible smartphones with hardware acceleration, Mnemo supports running a local language model directly on your device.

  • ✓ Computations execute on local silicon
  • ✓ Zero network transmission during local inference
  • ✓ Subject to model compatibility and phone RAM
Smarter Workspace

Ask & Memory AI

Dedicated Ask and Memory sections help you query your notes, discover cross-topic links, and synthesize research across your archive.

  • ✓ Answers grounded strictly in your notes
  • ✓ Direct citations pointing back to source passages
  • ✓ Complete user control: Enable or disable at will

Data Ownership Guarantee: Your notes are stored locally on your device. You are never locked into an active subscription just to access your data. We strongly recommend regular exports and device backups to safeguard against device loss or app-data clearing.
